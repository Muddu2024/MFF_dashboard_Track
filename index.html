Best Eval Strategy

  • Avoid the 2-day max-speed pass unless you are very consistent. $1,500 + $1,500 passes mathematically, but it forces large daily risk.
  • Safer target: aim for around $600–$800/day over 4–5 green trading days.
  • Risk cap idea: stop for the day after -$300 to -$500, even though there is no official daily loss limit.
  • Consistency rule: keep your best day under 50% of total profit. Example: if your best day is $1,200, your total profit should be at least $2,400.
  • Do not oversize: start with micros or 1 mini until you are comfortably green. Passing is less important than not breaching.
